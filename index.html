Why use GitGet?

No More Release Hunting

Tired of scrolling through dozens of files in a "Release" tab just to find the right installer? GitGet identifies your system and highlights exactly what you need, instantly.

Pure & Authentic

We value your security. GitGet doesn't host any files; every download link comes straight from the official GitHub servers, so you're always getting the real deal from the developers.

Lightweight & Fast

No sign-ups, no tracking, and zero bloat. We've built GitGet to be as fast as possible so you can find your tools and get back to work without any distractions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GitGet safe to use?

Absolutely. Think of us as a bridge, not a warehouse. When you click download, your browser talks directly to GitHub. We never touch your files or your data.

How does it detect my system?

We use standard browser info to guess your OS (Windows, macOS, or Linux). If we're off, you can easily switch it manually using the badge at the top!

Is this service free?

Yes, GitGet is 100% free and open-source. It's built by the community, for the community, to make great software accessible to everyone.
